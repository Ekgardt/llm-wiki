# Backlog to the goal — 2026-08-30

The goal: a memory system that beats every competitor, including
codebase-memory-mcp and Graphify, on efficiency **and** quality.

Efficiency is close. Quality is a factor away: 0.396 here against 0.878–0.944
published on LongMemEval. This backlog is ordered by how much of that factor
each item can move, not by how easy it is.

Evidence for every claim below:
`docs/research/2026-08-30-what-actually-closes-the-quality-gap.md`.

---

## M — Measurement, before anything else

Nothing below is provable until this is done. Five runs here span 0.07 at
n = 50; the field reports 3-run means over all 500 questions. Any item shipped
against the current measurement is a guess wearing a number.

### M1. A stand that can resolve a real difference
Stratified n = 200, three runs per arm, paired seeds, per-category accuracy with
its spread. Decision rule stated **before** the run: an arm wins only if the
mean gain exceeds the observed spread of the baseline arm.
**Gate:** the same arm run twice differs by less than the smallest effect we
intend to claim.

### M2. Answer-path token cost
We have never measured tokens per answered question. The comparison points are
~6 900 added tokens (Mem0) and ~720 (Supermemory). Until this exists, "we are
efficient" is a claim about tool-call tokens against cbm and nothing else.
**Gate:** tokens per query reported per category alongside accuracy, every run.

### M3. LoCoMo and BEAM-1M harnesses
Two of the three benchmarks the field reports on have never been run here.
**Gate:** both produce a scored report with the same reporting shape as the
LongMemEval harness.

## Q — Quality: the architecture, not the components

The 56-configuration grid in arXiv 2605.04897 moved accuracy by 3.2 points
across 7 embedders × 8 rerankers. Query expansion bought 1.0. Against a gap of
forty points, component choice is not the lever. These three are.

### Q1. Retrieve at turn granularity — the largest single item
Our unit is a heading span of a page: for a session, the whole session, ~10 KB
against a 28 672-byte budget, so a **median of two** units reach the answer
model. The reference pipeline retrieves **messages**, reranks 100, and hands the
answerer **10**. A multi-session question needs two sessions; a temporal one
needs a date from one and a fact from another. Two slots on one page cannot
answer either, however good the ranking.

Split captured sessions at their timestamped entries in `corpus_snapshot`, carry
the parent page and its heading ancestry on each chunk for context, and let the
budget buy breadth.

**Gate:** median evidence units reaching the answer model ≥ 8; multi-session and
temporal accuracy up; **single-session categories not down** — they are strong
today precisely because the whole session arrives as one unit, and that is the
risk this item carries.

### Q2. The reranker always runs, over 100 candidates
`should_rerank` fires only on disagreement, closeness, or explicit intent;
`DEFAULT_RERANK_DEPTH` is 20; and no model is configured unless
`LLMWIKI_RERANKER_MODEL` and a pinned revision are both set — so in a default
install it never runs. The reference designs run it on every query over the top
100 and take the top 10. Cost is 100–300 ms on CPU for 50 candidates with a 22M
cross-encoder; the reported lift is +5 to +15 NDCG@10.

Ship a pinned local model (`ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`, 22M, as the floor;
`bge-reranker-v2-m3` as the default where it fits the budget), depth 100 → 10,
on by default, with the existing optional-stage timeout as the fence.

**Gate:** reranker participation reported per query; p95 added latency inside
the optional-stage budget; accuracy measured with M1.

### Q3. Time as a modelled dimension
Two halves. The cheap one: a conditional score boost on temporal intent — the
reference multiplies by 1.3. The real one: **bitemporal facts**, each claim
carrying valid time (true in the world) and transaction time (recorded here), so
"what was true in June" and "what did I believe in June" are different queries.
Reported gains on the time-dependent categories are the field's largest:
+29.6 temporal, +23.1 multi-hop.

Do the boost first — it is small and measurable. Bitemporal claims are a schema
decision and need the owner's sign-off before any code.

**Gate:** temporal and knowledge-update accuracy, measured with M1, boost and
bitemporal reported separately so the cheap half is not credited with the
expensive half's gain.

### Q4. A current-state view over the additive store
Knowledge update is the hardest category for an additive memory, and the field's
answer is not deletion but a computed current-state view over preserved history.
This vault already supersedes rather than deletes, so the store is right and the
**view** is missing.

**Gate:** knowledge-update accuracy, with the superseded page still retrievable
when asked for history.

## E — Efficiency and operations

### E1. The undo trail is quadratic
Every journal append stores a full before and a full after copy. `run/` reached
**4.4 GB** in ten days on this vault, and journal rotation caps the file, not the
per-append cost. Inside the 30-day undo contract, so this is a design decision,
not a bug — and it needs the owner's sign-off because it touches the transaction
contract.

**Gate:** bytes written per checkpoint, before and after.

### E2. Reservation lifetime
A reservation that never commits blocks its sequence forever, and
`project_checkpoints` carries no timestamp, so a dead reservation cannot be told
from a live one by age. Today the `checkpoints` health finding reports it and a
human decides. Owner's call.

### E3. Two decisions still open from the audit
The permanent health finding raised by a single quarantine record, and whether
`MAX_OPERATIONAL_ROWS` should exist at all.

## What is deliberately **not** here

Swapping the embedder, tuning RRF weights, query expansion, a second graph, a
vector database, a GPU. The evidence says these are worth a few points between
them, and the reference system reaches 87.8 without any of them — a single
SQLite file on commodity CPU. Our constraints are not what is holding the number
down.

---

## Order of work

M1 and M2 first, together — they are cheap and everything after them is
unprovable without them. Then Q1, which is the largest single item and the one
whose risk the measurement exists to watch. Then Q2, which is nearly free once
Q1 has widened the candidate set. Then Q3's cheap half, M3, Q4, and Q3's
bitemporal half behind a decision. E1 and E2 are the owner's to schedule.

## M2 — сделано частично, 2026-08-31

Измерение существовало, но считало половину: `est_prompt_tokens` — это
`len(prompt) / 4` по **пользовательскому** промпту, а системный промпт
записывался отдельным полем и в стоимость не входил. Все прежние цифры этого
стенда были занижены. Числа, которые публикует поле, — целиком контекстные.

Добавлено `est_total_prompt_tokens` и колонка `total_tokens` в сводке; старое
поле сохранено, чтобы прежние отчёты читались рядом с новыми.

**Где мы на самом деле, по последнему прогону n=50:** 4 220 оценочных токенов
пользовательского промпта плюс 668 системного — около **4 900 на вопрос**.
Против ~6 900 у Mem0 и ~720 добавленных у Supermemory.

То есть по стоимости ответа мы **уже дешевле Mem0** и заметно дороже
Supermemory, а по точности 0.396 против 0.944 и 0.95 Recall@15. Это уточняет
цель: экономить токены дальше сейчас незачем — вся дистанция в качестве.

Оба числа — `chars / 4`, а не токенизатор. Настоящий счёт требует сетевого
вызова, которого этот путь не делает; расхождение с реальным BPE для английской
прозы обычно в пределах десятой части, но это оценка, и так и написано.

## M1 — стенд готов, 2026-08-31

`benchmark/compare_arms.py` считает вердикт по правилу, записанному до прогона:
`docs/research/2026-08-31-a-decision-rule-stated-before-the-run.md`.

Правило: рука выигрывает разряд только если её среднее превосходит среднее
базовой руки больше, чем на собственный разброс базовой в этом разряде. Падение
больше того же разброса — проигрыш, и проигрыш блокирует изменение, что бы ни
делало общее среднее. Всё прочее — «разницы не измерено», а это не «разницы
нет» и не выигрыш. Разброс — `max - min` по прогонам руки: прямое утверждение
о том, насколько число гуляет, когда ничего не менялось.

Стенд говорит и о том, чего у него не хватает: если рука прогнана меньше трёх
раз, он пишет, что всякий вердикт выше — первый взгляд, а не измерение.

**Проверено на настоящих отчётах**, три базовых прогона против вчерашней
упаковки:

```
knowledge-update            0.3333 ±0.0      0.5714 ±0.0     win
multi-session               0.0833 ±0.0      0.1818 ±0.0     win
single-session-assistant    0.9333 ±0.2         0.8 ±0.0     no difference measured
temporal-reasoning          0.1581 ±0.0129   0.1538 ±0.0     no difference measured
overall                     0.3289 ±0.0685   0.3958 ±0.0     no difference measured
weak: candidate ran fewer than 3 times
```

То есть вчерашний прирост по общей точности — 0.067 против разброса базовой
0.0685 — не дотягивает, ровно как я и говорил; а два разряда, где базовая не
шевелилась ни разу в трёх прогонах, показали выигрыш. Кандидат прогнан один
раз, и стенд об этом сообщает сам.

Остаётся из M: три прогона кандидата на n=200 (это ночная работа) и стенды
LoCoMo и BEAM.
