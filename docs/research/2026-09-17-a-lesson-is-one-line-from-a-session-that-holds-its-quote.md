# A lesson is written on one line, from the session that holds its quote

Dated 2026-09-17. Finding M-A13 (first item) of the third audit (low, confirmed by reading
and by a test). The research before the fix.

Files: `scripts/episode_consolidation.py`,
`tests/test_a_lesson_is_one_line_from_a_session_that_holds_its_quote.py`.

## What was found

- `episode_consolidation._string_field` strips the ends of a model-supplied string and
  keeps everything inside it. `text`, `trigger`, `session` and `quote` are then written
  verbatim into the daily log by `render_block`.
- A daily log is line-structured: `## [HH:MM:SS]` begins an entry and a `- ` line at
  column 0 begins an item. A reply whose `text` contains a newline followed by such a line
  adds an entry the consolidation never produced. The reply is shaped by session content,
  which is untrusted (tool output, fetched pages), so this is a path from a web page to a
  durable note.
- `quote` must occur in the day's records, but a record is multi-line, so a grounded quote
  can itself span a line break and carry a heading.
- `session` is whatever the model wrote. It is not checked against the sessions that were
  read, and it becomes the `Source:` line the compile pipeline cites.

## Practice on this date

- "Improper Output Handling refers specifically to insufficient validation, sanitization,
  and handling of the outputs generated by large language models before they are passed
  downstream to other components and systems." The first remedy: "Treat the model as any
  other user, adopting a zero-trust approach, and apply proper input validation on
  responses coming from the model to backend functions."
  ([OWASP LLM05:2025 Improper Output Handling](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm052025-improper-output-handling/)).
- For a line-structured sink the validation is structural: a field that is one line in the
  format is made one line before it is written, so no content can end the line.

## The decision

- Every field of a lesson is written as one line: runs of whitespace, line breaks included,
  become one space. The quote is checked against the records as the model gave it, and
  only then folded to one line for writing.
- The session of a lesson is the record that contains its quote, found by the code. What
  the model wrote in `session` is no longer used for the `Source:` line.
- The two other items of M-A13 (the 240-record day ceiling and a record written after its
  day was consolidated) are left: both change what a day means and are named in the
  report. No path, environment variable or contract changes.
