# Project history: model providers and data-loss prevention, part 2 of 2

This packet owns 15 dated research, plan, audit or report records about model providers and data-loss prevention from 2026-10-02 to 2026-11-03.

is not rewritten to follow the code; a later decision supersedes it with a new dated record.

in their file name, then by date, to give a reader one bounded place to look; the grouping is
navigation and makes no claim about a record's content.

The owned files and the complete maintenance context are listed in the `-context.json`
