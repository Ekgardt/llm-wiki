## Status, 2026-09-08 night: the owner rejected the numbers, and rightly

"19k–58k tokens is catastrophic", then twice "this looks like a crutch". The
three whole entries and the three-on-a-second-pass were numbers chosen under
pressure. They are gone. What replaced them, each with its sources in
`docs/research/2026-09-08-small-keys-large-values-and-a-loop-that-stops.md`:

| crutch | replacement | where |
|---|---|---|
| whole entries by default | the turn is the unit found; a user turn is a 300-byte key delivered with its reply | `corpus_snapshot.TURN_MARKERS`, `query_memory._with_partner` |
| reading every byte of a reply | the reply pruned to the sentences the dual encoder scores against the question, byte-exact | `scripts/evidence_pruning.py` |
| a wider second pass | a loop that stops when a step adds no instance, at most three steps | `query_memory._second_look`, `MAX_COUNT_STEPS` |
| widening a chunk to its page | a chunk is delivered as itself | `query_memory._compiled_context` |

Branch `work-rounds` (on top of tasks 1–6 and the working field). Measured
next, both arms, against run 1 and the target of under 8k prompt tokens per
question.
