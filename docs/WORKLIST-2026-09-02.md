## Where the losses actually are — measured 2026-09-02, n=200 seed 101

Read off `benchmark/longmemeval-claimlevel-n200-r1.json` and its judged rows,
using the **labelled** answer session rather than the substring flag.

**Retrieval is not the bottleneck.** Of 186 questions that have a labelled
answer session, retrieval brought it back for 162 (87%), ranked first in 143 of
them. Almost every loss happens after the right evidence is already in hand.

**87 questions outside the abstention category produced no answer. 62 of them
had the answer session retrieved.** Their breakdown:

| cause | count | status |
|---|---|---|
| one unresolvable citation killed the document | 18 | fixed in `772cbf0`, unmeasured |
| provider replied with a valid JSON fence plus trailing prose | 15 | open — 13 of the 15 are recoverable |
| refused despite having the evidence | 22 | open — needs the prompt looked at |
| conflicting / time-scope / schema | 7 | open |

### Next two items, in order

1. **Accept the first fenced JSON block anywhere in the reply.**
   `_unfenced` in `scripts/query_memory.py` only unwraps a response that is
   *exactly* one fence. Measured: 15 complete, correct answers were discarded
   for having a sentence of commentary after the closing backticks; 10 parse
   from a leading fence and 3 more from a fence that follows prose. Every claim
   is still gated afterwards, so trailing prose is discarded, not trusted.

2. **Find out why 22 questions refuse with the evidence retrieved.** No
   hypothesis yet. The reply text is recorded in `raw_reply`, so this is
   readable rather than guessable.

### A measurement caveat that must not be repeated

`gold_in_prompt` is a substring test for the gold answer inside the prompt. On
the answered rows it reads False for 39 questions of which **29 were judged
correct** — it misses roughly three quarters of the cases where the evidence was
in fact present. It is a lower bound and nothing more. The size of the
"retrieval missed it" bucket must be taken from the labelled answer session, not
from that flag. This is the fourth defect of this family; see
`docs/research/2026-09-01-a-category-graded-by-the-wrong-question.md`.
